Terms

Terms for the early public release in Gomoh and Dhanbad.

Raahi Learning helps people discover and manage local learning relationships. By using Raahi, you agree to use the platform for genuine education-related purposes and to provide information you are authorized to provide.

Accounts and authority

Your sign-in identifies your Account. Access to a learner, Class, organization or privileged workspace depends on the current Raahi relationship or permission for that resource. You must not attempt to access another person's private learning information.

Learners and guardians

Parents and guardians should create or manage learner relationships only where they are authorized to do so. Learner history belongs to the learner identity, not permanently to one manager's login.

Teachers and organizations

Teachers and education organizations are responsible for keeping public profile, availability, fee and offering information accurate. Raahi may display verification status where a verification process has actually been completed; absence of a badge is not an adverse judgment.

Community conduct

Use Community features for respectful, useful learning discussion. Do not publish harassment, impersonation, unlawful material, personal contact details where Raahi asks you not to, or content that compromises learner safety.

Enquiries, Classes and payments

Raahi records platform learning relationships and communications where applicable. Unless a feature explicitly says otherwise, the current service does not represent that Raahi has collected or guaranteed an off-platform fee or service outcome.

Sponsored opportunities

Sponsored education content is identified as sponsored. Advertising does not change organic access permissions or provide advertisers with private learner data.

Safety and moderation

Reports request review and are not proof of wrongdoing. Raahi may restrict public discovery, contact or other features when needed for safety, integrity or service operations.

Early release availability

Raahi is in its early public release in Gomoh and Dhanbad. Features may change, pause or be limited while we validate the service. We will avoid silently rewriting existing private learning history when operational changes are made.

Contact

For support, contact support@myraahi.co.in.

Privacy

See the Raahi Learning Privacy notice for how information is handled.

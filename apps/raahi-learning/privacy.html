Privacy

Effective for the early public release in Gomoh and Dhanbad.

Raahi Learning is a local learning network designed to help learners, parents, teachers and education providers connect while keeping private learning relationships separate from public discovery.

What we collect

We collect the information needed to operate your Raahi account and the features you choose to use. This can include your name, Google account identifier and email, optional profile information, learner relationships, teaching information, enquiries, Classes, messages, community activity and files you intentionally upload.

Google sign-in

Google is used to authenticate you. Raahi does not use your Google profile to decide your learning roles, permissions or relationships. Your Raahi profile is stored separately. If a Google profile photo is offered, it is not published automatically.

Phone confirmation

During the current controlled Gomoh and Dhanbad pilot, Google sign-in is sufficient and Raahi does not require phone confirmation. If Raahi introduces phone or WhatsApp confirmation later for selected trust-sensitive actions, it will remain an additional security check rather than the primary sign-in method.

Public and private information

Teacher and organization information is public only when the relevant Raahi profile or learning option is intentionally published. Learner profiles, private Classes, enquiries, messages and learning history are relationship-scoped and are not a public learner directory.

How we use information

We use information to provide account access, local discovery, learning relationships, Classes, communications, safety controls, moderation, auditability and other features you request. We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

Sponsored content

Raahi may show clearly identified education-related sponsored opportunities. Sponsored content does not grant advertisers access to private learning records or private messages.

Retention and account controls

Some information can be edited, paused or closed through Raahi controls. Certain shared learning, safety or audit records may need to remain where deleting them would break another person's learning history, an unresolved responsibility, or platform integrity.

Security

Raahi uses server-side authorization and database access controls so opening a page or knowing an identifier does not itself grant access to private information.

Children and family use

Parents or guardians can manage learner profiles. Raahi separates the login Account from the Learner identity so learning history can remain attached to the learner while management relationships change.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact support@myraahi.co.in.

Changes

We may update this notice as the pilot develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page before broader rollout.

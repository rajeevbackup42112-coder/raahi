# Raahi Learning Network — Market Activation & Seeding Blueprint V1.4

Status: **MARKET-ACTIVATION DESIGN — NO FAKE TRACTION**

## 1. Objective

Make every newly launched Location useful and lively before organic network effects exist, while preserving Raahi’s legitimacy.

Principle:

> **Seed usefulness, never fake popularity.**

Raahi may create editorial value, assist genuine users, curate public factual information and recruit initial supply. It must not invent independent people, demand, enquiries, ratings, testimonials or investor traction.

## 2. Seed engines

Raahi should bootstrap each Location through four complementary engines:

1. **Raahi Desk** — platform-authored local learning questions, guides, polls and useful updates.
2. **Founding Supply** — genuine teachers/coaching organisations concierge-onboarded with permission.
3. **Local Learning Guide** — factual education events, opportunities, resources and locality knowledge.
4. **Assisted Participation** — real teachers/students/parents helped by Raahi to publish their genuine need or offering.

## 3. Raahi Desk launch pack per Location

Before actively promoting a Location, prepare roughly 20–30 useful lightweight items rather than a handful of filler posts.

Suggested mix:

- 5 parent questions/guides;
- 5 student questions/polls;
- 4 teacher-oriented discussions;
- 3 local opportunity/event/resource posts;
- 2–3 cross-location or travel-for-learning discussions where locally relevant;
- 2 recurring-series starters such as `Ask a Teacher` and `This week in learning`.

Examples for Gomoh/Dhanbad:

- “Gomoh students who travel to Dhanbad for tuition: which subjects make the trip worthwhile?”
- “What should parents ask before choosing a Class 10 Maths tutor?”
- “Teachers: what batch size works best for Classes 9–10?”
- “Home tuition, coaching or online—what works best for you?”

Raahi Desk may answer its own factual/guidance question openly as the platform. It must not impersonate a parent, student or teacher.

## 4. Founding Teacher / organisation programme

Target the first 15–30 genuine teachers across Gomoh and Dhanbad through assisted onboarding.

Raahi may help with profile setup, photo/logo upload, subjects/classes, teaching areas, short introduction and first learning option. The teacher must review/consent to published information.

Optional recognition:

**Founding Teacher · Gomoh** / **Founding Teacher · Dhanbad**

This badge means only that the teacher genuinely joined during the founding launch period. It is not a quality ranking, verification badge or endorsement.

For established coaching organisations, Raahi may consider minimal factual `Unclaimed listing` pages based on lawful public information, clearly labelled and claimable by the owner. Individual private tutors should not receive fabricated/unclaimed personal profiles.

## 5. Genuine demand activation

Learning Requests must represent genuine needs.

Use concierge activation instead of fabrication: ask early parents/students what they are actually looking for, help them write the request, let them approve it, and publish under their real Raahi ownership/privacy context.

Five genuine requests are more valuable than fifty invented ones.

## 6. Recurring local rituals

Use predictable community habits until organic posting takes over:

- Monday — `New learning options this week`;
- Wednesday — `Ask a Teacher`;
- Friday — `Opportunities & events`;
- Sunday — `This week in <Location>`.

Keep cadence useful rather than mechanically filling the feed. A ritual may be skipped when there is nothing valuable to publish.

## 7. WhatsApp-to-Raahi bridge

Early users will often communicate their need through WhatsApp or in person before they post it themselves.

With explicit permission, Raahi may assist them to turn that genuine information into a Raahi profile, learning option or Learning Request. The resulting content remains the user’s content—not a Raahi-created fictional persona.

## 8. Organic-transition metric

Track content provenance so Raahi can measure the transition from seeded utility to network effects.

Useful investor/operating metrics include:

- % platform-authored activity;
- % assisted genuine-user activity;
- % organic user-created activity;
- first organic post/request after Location launch;
- teacher response rate to genuine requests;
- cross-Location browsing/switching behaviour.

## 9. Hard anti-deception guardrails

Never fabricate or inflate:

- student/parent/teacher identities;
- Learning Requests;
- teacher availability;
- enquiries or conversations;
- reviews, ratings or testimonials;
- user/teacher/request counts;
- successful matches;
- urgency or scarcity;
- investor traction metrics.

Editorial seed content must be visibly attributable to Raahi Desk. Assisted content must have a genuine human source and permission.

## 10. Location activation readiness

Before actively promoting a new Location, aim for:

- enough genuine teacher/organisation supply that Explore is useful;
- an initial Raahi Desk content pack;
- accurate local opportunities/resources where available;
- at least a small number of genuine local participants invited to create the first requests/discussions;
- no misleading empty ad/module surfaces dominating Home;
- a clear plan for support/moderation during the first weeks.

The target is not to make a new Location look popular. The target is to make it **useful enough that the first real users have reasons to stay and contribute**.
